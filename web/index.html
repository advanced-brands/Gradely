Academic life is scattered.

Portals, group chats, notebooks, timetables, and announcements rarely live in one place. Deadlines slip. Attendance is a guess. Exam week arrives without a clear picture of what is done.

  • Forgotten assignment dates
  • Unclear attendance risk
  • Notes that cannot be found later
  • No weekly focus

One companion for the semester.

You enter your own courses and activities. Gradely organises them by semester, calculates attendance, surfaces upcoming work, and uses simple rules — not hype — to tell you what needs attention.

What Gradely helps you see

Attendance

Record classes and watch the percentage update. Get a warning when a course falls below your threshold.

Deadlines

Assignments, tests, and exams in one list, with overdue calculated from today’s date.

Progress

Activity progress from completed tasks — labelled honestly, not as a predicted grade.

Notes

Capture a topic against a course and find it again when revision starts.

Guidance

Short, rule-based recommendations when attendance slips or several deadlines land together.

Semester view

Courses, tasks, and notes stay inside the term you are actually living through.

How you get started

This website is the introduction. Gradely itself is an application on your computer. There is no cloud signup here, and your student number does not download your school records.

  1. 01 Open the Gradely application
  2. 02 Create your space — name, student number, institution, program, semester
  3. 03 Add the courses you are taking
  4. 04 Record attendance, tasks, and notes as the term goes on
  5. 05 Check the dashboard for deadlines and guidance

Take control of your semester.

Run Gradely on your computer. Your profile and academic data live in a local database — yours, not the institution’s.
